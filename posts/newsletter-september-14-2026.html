✈️🇨🇦 Flights from $199, New Aeroplan Promo & Last Day for Icelandair Sale!

Hey Travellers!

When I say points, you say promo! Points, promo! Points, promo!

Sorry…I know that was kind of lame. 😂

But it’s always an exciting time whenever Aeroplan launches a new points buying promotion!

Source: Aeroplan.

Until September 25th, the loyalty program is offering up to a 100% bonus when you purchase Aeroplan points.

Here is the full bonus structure being offered:

  • Buy 5,000–25,000 points: 50% bonus (2.50 cents per point, pre-tax)
  • Buy 30,000–70,000 points: 75% bonus (2.14 cents per point, pre-tax)
  • Buy 80,000+ points: 100% bonus (1.88 cents per point, pre-tax)

Aeroplan points are generally valued at around 2 cents per point. That makes the 100% bonus tier a solid deal, with points landing at roughly 1.88 cents each. The 50% and 75% tiers are less compelling on a straight cents per point basis, but if you only need a small top up to complete an Aeroplan redemption, they are still better than paying the regular rate.

On another note, this is a reminder that Icelandair’s sale from Canada to Iceland & Europe is ending tonight!

Icelandair tends to have these limited-time promotions every couple months and when they do, I notice a lot of great prices for getting over to Europe.

Yes, it’s true you’ll need to have a layover in Reykjavík. Sometimes this is just for a couple hours, and sometimes it’s for almost 24 hours…but this means you get to explore this beautiful city for a day, which I highly recommend!
