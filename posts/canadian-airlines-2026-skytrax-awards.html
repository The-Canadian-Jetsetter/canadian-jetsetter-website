Canadian Airlines Take Home Top Honours at the 2026 Skytrax World Airline Awards

It was a strong showing for Canada at this year's Skytrax World Airline Awards, with three of the country's carriers walking away with major titles.

The awards, presented in London and often described as the aviation industry's version of the Oscars, are decided by customer satisfaction surveys filled out by millions of travellers around the world over the course of the year.

For 2026, Air Canada, Air Transat, and Porter Airlines each earned recognition that puts Canadian aviation firmly in the global spotlight.

Header Image Source: Skytrax.

Air Canada Repeats as Best Airline in North America

Mark Nasr, COO of Air Canada receives the 2026 Best Airline in North America Award. Source: Skytrax.

Air Canada was named Best Airline in North America for the second year running, the tenth time the carrier has claimed the title since 2010. It also climbed two spots to rank 17th on the list of the World's Top 100 Airlines for 2026, making it the only North American carrier to crack the top 20.

Beyond that headline title, Air Canada collected a handful of other awards. Its Signature Suite at Toronto Pearson was recognized for the world's best business class lounge dining for the third straight year, and the same lounge took Best Business Class Lounge in North America. The airline also earned Best Cabin Crew in Canada and Best Economy Class Onboard Catering in North America.

Air Canada runs Signature Suite lounges for departing international passengers at both Toronto Pearson and Vancouver International, with menus built around Canadian ingredients and regional wines.

The award also caps a busy stretch of announcements from Air Canada. The airline unveiled its largest intercontinental expansion yet, adding five new summer 2027 non-stops (Guangzhou, Oslo, Shannon, Basel, and Dubrovnik) flown partly on the new Airbus A321XLR. Onboard, its fleet-wide "Glowing Hearted" cabins bring Canada's first narrowbody lie-flat business class, and a refreshed set of international bedding and amenity kits leans on Canadian partners Hunter Amenities and Sahajan.

Air Transat Doubles Up on Leisure Titles

Annick Guérard, CEO of Air Transat receives the 2026 World's Best Leisure Airline Award. Source: Skytrax.

Air Transat had one of the best days of any Canadian carrier, taking home two awards. It was crowned World's Best Leisure Airline, a title the Montreal-based airline has now earned eight times, and it also picked up Best Leisure Airline in North America, a regional crown it last held in 2022. Its previous world titles came in 2012, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2023, 2024, and 2025.

The leisure category is judged specifically on the quality of the onboard and on-the-ground experience for leisure travellers. Founded in Montreal in 1987, Air Transat flies to destinations across Europe, the Caribbean, the eastern United States, and North Africa, operating out of hubs in Montreal and Toronto with a workforce of roughly 5,000.

The recognition also lands during a period of rapid growth. Over the past year Air Transat has opened a wave of new direct routes from Montreal and Toronto, with summer additions to Tirana, Dakar, Reykjavík, Agadir, and Rio de Janeiro already flying, and winter service to Los Cabos, Las Palmas, Oranjestad, and Bridgetown all set to begin in December 2026.

Porter Makes It Three in a Row for Best Regional Airline

Edmond Eldebs, SVP & Chief Commercial Officer, Porter Airlines, receives the 2026 Best Regional Airline in North America Award. Source: Skytrax.

Porter Airlines took Best Regional Airline in North America for the third consecutive year. The category covers carriers that operate primarily or entirely within North America, and like the other titles it is based on the Skytrax passenger survey.

Porter has leaned on the same approach to economy travel since it launched in 2006, and it clearly continues to land with passengers. The formula includes no middle seats, complimentary beer and wine served in glassware, premium snacks, and free WiFi. Porter now serves 131 routes and 54 destinations, reaching every Canadian province while continuing to expand across the United States, the Caribbean, and Latin America. It is also one of only three North American carriers to hold a Skytrax Official 4-Star Rating.

That third straight win comes while Porter is firmly in expansion mode, favouring smaller Canadian airports over the big hubs. In 2026 alone it opened a new base at Montreal Metropolitan Airport (MET) with 12 domestic destinations, launched its first-ever flights to London, Ontario, with a year-round network promised by summer 2027, and signed a codeshare with British Airways that places a BA code on 17 of its Canadian routes, letting Avios collectors earn on the Porter leg.

The Big Global Winners in 2026

Source: Skytrax.

Canada was not the only story in London. Here are some of the marquee titles handed out this year:

  • Airline of the Year: Singapore Airlines
  • World's Best Economy Class: Singapore Airlines
  • World's Best Business Class: Qatar Airways
  • World's Best First Class: Air France
  • World's Best Cabin Crew: Cathay Pacific
  • World's Best Premium Economy Class: EVA Air
  • World's Best Low-Cost Airline: AirAsia
  • World's Best Long Haul Low-Cost Airline: Scoot
  • World's Cleanest Airline: STARLUX Airlines
  • Best Airline in Europe: Turkish Airlines
  • World's Best Airline Alliance: Oneworld

My Thoughts

What stands out to me is the range.

A full-service flag carrier, a leisure specialist, and a regional airline were each recognized for doing very different things well, which says a lot about the overall health of the options Canadians have at home.

Air Canada's climb into the global top 20 is especially notable given how competitive that list is, Air Transat quietly won two leisure titles in one night, and Porter stringing together a third straight regional win shows their formula is not a fluke.

The Bottom Line

Whether you are booking a business class seat with Air Canada, chasing some sun with Air Transat, or hopping between cities on Porter, this year's Skytrax results are a reminder that Canadian travellers have some seriously strong options close to home.

It is a good year to be flying Canadian.

Happy travels!
