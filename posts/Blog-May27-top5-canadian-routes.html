5 Exciting New Routes From Canada Launching in 2026

Canadian aviation is having a moment.

In just the past several months, we have seen a wave of new route announcements that move the needle for Canadian travellers. New long-haul destinations, first-ever connections, and a brand-new airport experience right here at home. Whether you are based in Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto, or Montreal, there is something on this list worth getting excited about.

Here are 5 exciting new routes from Canada launching in 2026.

1. Iberia: Toronto to Madrid (Starting June 13, 2026)

This one flew under the radar when it was announced last fall, but it deserves far more attention as the launch date approaches.

Iberia is launching nonstop service between Toronto Pearson and Madrid Barajas on June 13, 2026, operating five times weekly on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays. Up until now, Air Canada has been the only airline flying this route nonstop. Iberia's entry into the market brings real competition, and that is almost always good news for fares and seat availability.

The Toronto departure leaves at 17:10, which means you can work a full day, head to Pearson, and wake up early in Spain ready to go. And from Madrid, connectivity across Europe, North Africa, and Latin America is exceptional. Iberia also offers a Madrid stopover of up to nine nights, which is a smart way to build a multi-destination trip.

The aircraft is the Airbus A321XLR, which Iberia launched globally. It is a long-range narrowbody designed for exactly this kind of transatlantic route, featuring a modern Airspace cabin and strong fuel efficiency.

For points collectors, RBC Avion points transfer 1:1 to British Airways Avios, which can then be used to book Iberia flights. That makes this an especially interesting redemption opportunity heading into summer.

2. Porter Airlines: New Routes from MET Airport in Montreal (Starting June 15, 2026)

Source: MET Airport

This one is less about a single route and more about an entirely new airport experience.

Starting June 15, 2026, Porter Airlines will operate flights from Montreal Metropolitan Airport, also known as MET, to 12 destinations across Canada. MET is located in Longueuil, just 15 minutes from downtown Montreal, with a brand-new terminal and only nine gates. The result is a smaller, more streamlined airport experience with shorter lines, faster boarding, and a level of convenience that most Canadians have not seen from a commercial airport in a long time.

The route network Porter is launching from MET is coast-to-coast. Destinations include Toronto City and Toronto Pearson, Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Halifax, Winnipeg, Quebec City, St. John's, Charlottetown, Moncton, and Hamilton. Several routes operate daily or multiple times daily from launch.

For South Shore residents and anyone in greater Montreal who has dreaded the trek to Montreal Trudeau International Airport, this changes things in a practical and meaningful way. MET has been operating in various forms since 1925 and was renamed in 2023.

What it is becoming under Porter is something quite different from what Canada typically offers: a boutique, passenger-focused domestic hub that prioritizes the experience of actually getting on and off a plane.

3. Air Canada: Toronto and Montreal to Tenerife, Canary Islands (Starting October 25, 2026)

This might be the most interesting new route for Canadian sun seekers in years.

Air Canada will launch nonstop service from both Toronto Pearson and Montreal Trudeau International Airport to Tenerife South in the Canary Islands for the 2026-27 winter season. These will be the only nonstop flights between North America and the Canary Islands, full stop.

Tenerife is a year-round warm weather destination off the northwest coast of Africa that sits in a sweet spot between a traditional beach vacation and something with more texture. Volcanic landscapes, hiking, resort towns, and a different vibe than the usual Caribbean or Mexican offering. For Canadians looking to shake up their winter sun routine, this is a compelling option.

What makes the route especially noteworthy is the aircraft. Air Canada will operate the Tenerife flights with its new Airbus A321XLR, one of the most strategically significant planes entering its fleet. The aircraft features lie-flat seats in Signature Class and 168 Economy seats, and it is designed to make thinner long-haul routes economically viable. Tenerife is exactly the kind of destination this plane was built for, and it will likely not be the last interesting long-thin route we see from Air Canada as the XLR fleet grows.

The Toronto schedule operates Thursdays and Sundays from October 25 through April 29, 2027. Montreal operates Saturdays from October 31 through April 24, 2027. Plenty of flexibility to build a week-long trip or something longer.

4. WestJet: Calgary to Sao Paulo, Brazil (Starting November 8, 2026)

This is WestJet's 100th nonstop destination from Calgary, and it is a historic one.

On November 8, 2026, WestJet will begin three-weekly service between Calgary and Sao Paulo Guarulhos aboard its Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner. This will be the first ever nonstop connection between Western Canada and South America.

What makes this route more than just a city pair is the connectivity it unlocks. Sao Paulo is LATAM Airlines' largest hub, and WestJet and LATAM have a codeshare partnership. That means Calgary passengers could potentially reach Lima, Santiago, Buenos Aires, Rio de Janeiro, Montevideo, and more than 30 other South American destinations without routing through Toronto, Montreal, or a US gateway.

The 787-9 Dreamliner brings a lie-flat business cabin, premium economy, and economy, along with strong cargo capability. From a strategy perspective, this is WestJet cementing Calgary's identity as a long-haul hub rather than just a domestic and transborder operation. For Alberta-based travellers who have historically had to route east to get anywhere interesting, this is a significant shift.

5. Air Canada: Vancouver to Sapporo, Japan (Starting December 17, 2026)

This one is for anyone who has ever dreamed about skiing in Hokkaido.

Air Canada will launch seasonal nonstop service between Vancouver and Sapporo's Chitose Airport on December 17, 2026, operating three times weekly through March 25, 2027. This will be the only nonstop service linking Sapporo with anywhere in North America.

Sapporo is globally known for its snow quality, the famous Sapporo Snow Festival, world-class onsen, ramen, and one of Japan's most distinct regional cultures. For Canadian skiers in particular, Hokkaido has long been a bucket list destination, and until now getting there nonstop simply was not an option from this side of the Pacific.

Air Canada's 787 Dreamliner will operate the route with Signature Class, Premium Economy, and Economy. Ski equipment is included within standard baggage allowances on international fares, except Basic Economy, which is a meaningful perk for a route built around winter sports demand. Vancouver's geographic position also allows for among the fastest routing between North America and Sapporo, shaving over two hours off many one-stop alternatives.

For Aeroplan members, this is a new and highly sought-after redemption opportunity opening up. Japan is already one of the most popular Aeroplan destinations, and Sapporo adds a premium leisure angle on top of the traditional business travel gateway.

The Bottom Line

What stands out looking at this list together is how much of it represents firsts. First nonstop from Western Canada to South America. First nonstop from North America to Sapporo. First nonstop between Canada and the Canary Islands. First commercial flights from a revitalized downtown-adjacent Montreal airport.

For Canadian travellers, that means more options, fewer connecting flights, and more reasons to start planning trips that were previously too much of a hassle to seriously consider.

Which of these five are you most excited about?

Happy travels!
