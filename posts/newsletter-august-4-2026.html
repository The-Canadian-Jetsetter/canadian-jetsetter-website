✈️🇨🇦 Flights from $183 + WestJet Travel Update, Aeroplan Bonus Points Promo Reminder

Hey Travellers!

If you or someone you know is flying with WestJet, this is an important update to know about.

The airline has reached a tentative deal with the union representing its flight attendants and has now resumed flights. That said, it is only just beginning to ramp its schedule back up, so be prepared for some potential delays.

For the most up-to-date information on this situation, check WestJet’s website using the button below.

Click here to visit WestJet’s website →

Now onto the world of Aeroplan. This is a reminder that you have until August 12th to take advantage of their bonus points promotion.

This is a targeted offer, so it varies by member. The top tier is up to a 110% bonus on Aeroplan points (up to 100%, plus an extra 10% for Aeroplan credit cardholders), while the general offer is up to an 85% bonus.

The move here is the same as always: buy only when you have a redemption in mind. A quick top-up before booking an itinerary can save you a lot of money versus paying cash, especially in a premium cabin.

The offer ends on August 12th, 2026. Head to your account, refresh the Buy Points page, and see what bonus you were given.

Click here to learn more! →

And now… the actual flight deals. Think round-trip fares from $183 in Canada, $331 to southern destinations, $588 to Europe, $779 to Asia, and Aeroplan redemptions starting at 15,800 points + fees one-way.

As always, prices and availability can change quickly. If something lines up with your 2026 travel plans, it is worth taking a closer look sooner rather than later.
