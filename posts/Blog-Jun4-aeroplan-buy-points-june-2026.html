Aeroplan's Latest Deal: Up to 30% Discount on Points

Aeroplan has launched a new limited-time promotion: get up to a 30% discount when you buy or gift Aeroplan points.

Current Promotion Details

Here's how the discount tiers break down:

  • Buy 3,000 – 14,000 points: Get a 15% discount
  • Buy 15,000 – 29,000 points: Get a 20% discount
  • Buy 30,000 – 35,000 points: Get a 25% discount
  • Buy 40,000 – 250,000 points: Get a 30% discount

Outside of promotional periods, Aeroplan sells points at a base rate of 3.75 cents per point (CAD). During this promotion, you could acquire points for as low as 2.62 cents per point (CAD) with the 30% discount.

Keep in mind that some members may receive different tier breakdowns, so your offer could look slightly different from the one above.

Purchase Limits

For this promotion, Aeroplan members can purchase a minimum of 3,000 points and up to 250,000 points per transaction, with a calendar year maximum of 1,000,000 points for purchases and 500,000 points for gifting.

Is It Worth Buying?

Honestly, this promotion offers less value compared to the up to 90% bonus promo that just ended on May 31st.

Outside of promotional periods, Aeroplan sells points at a base rate of 3.75 cents per point (CAD). With a 30% discount, you're looking at roughly 2.62 cents per point at the best tier. That's a decent rate, but nowhere near what the bonus promo was offering.

My recommendation: only buy if you have a specific redemption in mind and need to top up your balance to make it happen. If you're thinking about buying points speculatively or just to stockpile, I'd hold off and wait for a stronger promo.

Who Should Buy Aeroplan Points?

If you're strategic about travel rewards, buying points during a strong promotion can be a game-changer for your travel plans. Here are the key scenarios where purchasing points makes sense, especially during a good promotion (like up to 100% bonus points, which we've seen in the past):

Reach Your Dream Destination Faster

Are you just a few thousand points short of that trip to Europe or Asia you've been planning? Instead of waiting months to accumulate more points through everyday spending, you can purchase exactly what you need and book your trip now. This is especially valuable for those larger, dream trips that require significant point balances.

Never Miss Last-Minute Getaways

We've all been there — an incredible flight deal pops up, but your Aeroplan balance is too low to take advantage of it. When you have the ability to buy points instantly, you're always ready for spontaneous travel opportunities. Don't let a low balance stop you from jumping on time-sensitive deals or last-minute adventures.

Gift Points to a Loved One

Short on points yourself but want to help someone else travel sooner? You can purchase points directly for another Aeroplan member's account — they'll receive both the base points and the bonus points. A great way to give the gift of travel.

Treat It Like a Travel Savings Fund

Think of buying Aeroplan points the same way you'd save up for any major purchase. You can strategically top up your account throughout the year, especially during promotions like this one, building your "travel fund" for future adventures. It's a proactive approach to ensuring you have the points you need when the perfect travel opportunity arises.

How Long Does It Take to Receive Points?

Once your purchase is complete, Aeroplan generally posts points to your account within 72 hours. In most cases it happens faster than that, but it's good to keep that window in mind if you're buying points for a redemption you're hoping to book quickly.

The Bottom Line

This is a decent promotion if you need a top-up, but it's not the kind of deal worth going out of your way for. The recent 90% bonus promo was significantly stronger, and promotions like that do come around again.

If you have a specific trip in mind and are just short on points, go for it. Otherwise, it's worth being patient and waiting for the next big one.
