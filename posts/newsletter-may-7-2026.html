Hey Travellers!

Icelandair has a limited-time promotion from Canada to Europe for this Fall, getting you to Iceland and mainland Europe for lower prices in all classes. You'll notice some deals in today's newsletter with prices from:

  • Halifax to Europe from $577 CAD.
  • Toronto to Europe from $647 CAD.
  • Vancouver to Europe from $698 CAD.

On another note, here is some exciting news for Canadian travellers!

Porter Airlines announced that you can now book All-Inclusive vacation packages that bundle Porter non-stop flights with Air Transat's hotel and ground transfer expertise into one clean booking.

As of this moment, the available packages depart from Toronto, Ottawa, and Hamilton for travel from November 1st, 2026 onwards, covering four destinations:

  • Cancun, Mexico
  • Puerto Vallarta, Mexico
  • Nassau, The Bahamas
  • Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands

In addition to this, Porter announced some new Winter 2026-27 routes from various Canadian cities, including their first international flights from Alberta.
