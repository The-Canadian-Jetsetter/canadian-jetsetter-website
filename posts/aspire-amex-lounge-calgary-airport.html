I Got an Exclusive First Look Inside the New Aspire Amex Lounge in Calgary

I just got an exclusive first look inside the brand new Aspire Amex Lounge at Calgary International Airport, and I am happy to report that it does not disappoint. The lounge opened its doors today on September 16, 2026, and I was lucky enough to be there for the grand opening.

The first thing that struck me was the vibe. I can only describe it as swanky ski chalet chic. There is tons of natural light pouring in, incredible views out onto the tarmac, and plants everywhere you look. It is the kind of space where you actually want to arrive early for your flight.

Images of the lounge and its offerings are sourced from AMEX Canada / Aspire.

The Food and Drink

I got to sample pretty much everything, and the food is excellent. There is a buffet, tableside ordering, and grab and go options, so no matter how much time you have before your flight, there is something that works. American Express Platinum Card holders also get exclusive complimentary items, which is a nice touch.

The bar was next level too. The team was making cocktails and mocktails to order, and both were fantastic. Even the amenities are all Aesop, so your hands will be very happy after you wash them.

The Platinum Perk Worth Knowing About

Here is the part that matters most if you carry the card. Amex Platinum Card holders get priority entry to this lounge. That is a massive perk, and one more reason I love my Platinum Card.

I also had the chance to hear from executives at the Calgary airport, American Express, and Aspire about what this space means for travellers passing through Calgary. The theme was consistent. Lounge access has become a core part of the value Amex wants to offer, and the strong turnout at the Montréal lounge is what gave everyone the confidence to expand into Calgary and beyond.

Part of a Bigger Expansion

The Calgary lounge is part of a larger push by American Express and Aspire, which is Swissport’s airport hospitality business, to bring more of these lounges to Canada. The first co-branded lounge opened in the domestic departures area of Montréal-Trudeau back in September 2025, and it has already seen more than 50,000 Cardmember visits. That response is a big part of why the partnership is expanding.

The Calgary lounge is open now for travellers departing to domestic destinations. Eligible American Express Cardmembers receive priority lounge and waitlist access, along with that mix of buffet offerings and tableside ordering.

And there is more coming. A new Aspire Amex Lounge is set to land at Montréal-Trudeau in early 2027, this time serving travellers departing to the United States.

For a bit of wider context, the Aspire partnership is just one piece of the American Express lounge offering. Through the American Express Global Lounge Collection, eligible Cardmembers have access to more than 1,550 airport lounges across 140 countries.

My Thoughts

I have been in a lot of airport lounges, and this one immediately stands out. The design does a lot of the heavy lifting. The natural light and the tarmac views make it feel calm rather than crowded, which is not something I can say about every lounge. The made to order bar and the Aesop amenities are the kind of details that tell you real thought went into the space.

The one thing worth keeping in mind is that the Calgary lounge is set up for domestic departures. If you are flying to the United States or somewhere international out of Calgary, this particular lounge will not be part of your routing. For domestic flying, though, it is a fantastic addition, and the priority entry for Platinum Card holders is what pushes it from nice to have into something I will actually use.

The Bottom Line

If you hold an Amex Platinum Card and you fly domestically out of Calgary, the new Aspire Amex Lounge is a real upgrade to your pre-flight routine, and priority entry means you are not left waiting to get in. With a transborder lounge coming to Montréal in early 2027, and the existing domestic lounge there already past 50,000 visits, it is clear this partnership is only getting started. I am already looking forward to using the Calgary space more often.

Happy travels!
