Finnair Is Offering Up to a 50% Bonus on Purchased Avios

Finnair has launched a buy Avios promotion, and it is one worth a close look if you have a specific redemption in mind. From September 21 to October 5, 2026, you can earn up to a 50% bonus when you purchase Avios.

Note: This promotion is only for Finnair Plus members who joined the program before September 18, 2026. To see the promotional pricing and bonus rates, you must log in to your Finnair Plus account. You also need a minimum balance of 50 Avios in your account for this offer to appear, so if your balance is empty the promo will not show up. Purchased Avios post to your account within one to three days.

The size of the bonus scales with how many Avios you buy, topping out at 50% on the largest purchases. Below I will break down the tiers, whether buying actually makes sense, and the redemptions where Finnair Avios shines for Canadians.

How the Bonus Works

Source: Finnair

The more Avios you buy in a single purchase, the larger the bonus you receive. Here is the full bonus structure for this promotion.

Avios purchased Bonus
7,000 – 24,000 30%
25,000 – 54,000 35%
55,000 – 89,000 40%
90,000 – 200,000 50%

A few things to keep in mind before you buy:

  • Transactions must be completed between September 21 and October 5, 2026 to earn the bonus.
  • You must have been a Finnair Plus member before September 18, 2026, and hold a minimum balance of 50 Avios.
  • You can buy or receive a maximum of 200,000 Avios per calendar year. The bonus Avios do not count toward that limit.
  • Purchased Avios are not refundable, and they do not count toward Finnair Plus tier status.

Is Buying Avios Worth It?

Finnair Avios points are generally valued at around 2 cents (CAD). Even at the top 50% bonus tier, the effective cost of buying Avios comes out to about 2 cents each, which means you would be paying roughly what they are worth rather than picking them up at a discount.

So what is the point of buying? Not to bank Avios for a rainy day, unless you fly using Avios frequently and would like to purchase some points while there's a live promo. The reason to act on a sale like this is a single, high-value redemption, where each Avios you bought at 2 cents pulls far more than that in return. I will walk through two of the best below.

It is also worth knowing that Avios is not exclusive to Finnair. It is the shared currency of several programs, including Aer Lingus AerClub, the British Airways Club, Iberia Club, and Qatar Airways Privilege Club. That flexibility is part of what makes Avios useful, but Finnair is one of the standout ways to spend it from Canada.

Best Redemption: Finnair From Toronto to Helsinki

The best reason to care about Finnair Avios from Canada is the direct Finnair flight between Toronto (YYZ) and Helsinki (HEL). Finnair guarantees award space on its own flights to and from North America: at least four seats in economy, and at least two seats in premium economy and business class, all at fixed one-way rates.

Cabin Avios (one way)
Economy 30,000
Premium economy 43,500
Business 62,500

Business class is where this redemption gets exciting. At 3.38 cents per Avios, you are getting well above valuation, and you are doing it in a lie-flat business class seat for a fraction of the $4,661 CAD cash price.

Another Great Redemption: Cathay Pacific to Hong Kong

Another excellent way to spend Finnair Avios is on Cathay Pacific between Canada and Hong Kong (HKG), flying out of Toronto or Vancouver. Toronto tends to price higher in cash, since it is farther and has fewer direct connections to Asia than Vancouver, which makes an Avios redemption even more appealing from there.

Cathay Pacific business class award space is hard to come by, but when it does open up, the rate through Finnair Avios is outstanding: just 85,000 Avios one way from Vancouver or Toronto to Hong Kong.

More often you will find economy award space, at 35,000 Avios one way plus fees. That is still a strong deal, and Cathay Pacific has one of the best economy cabins in the world.

How to Get Avios

You do not need to fly Finnair to build up Avios. For Canadians, the most accessible path is a two-step transfer through British Airways.

Amex Membership Rewards (via British Airways)

American Express Membership Rewards points transfer to British Airways Avios at a 1:1 ratio, and from there you can move them into Finnair Plus. Transferring between the British Airways Club and Finnair Plus is quick and easy:

  • Link your Finnair Plus and British Airways Club accounts.
  • Select which account you would like to transfer Avios from or to.
  • Enter the amount of Avios you would like to transfer.
  • Confirm your selection, and the Avios transfer immediately.

You can move Avios between your Finnair Plus and British Airways Club accounts as often as you like.

RBC Avion (via British Airways)

RBC Avion points transfer to British Airways Avios at a 1:1 ratio, and from there you can combine them into Finnair Plus. RBC periodically runs 30% transfer bonuses to British Airways Avios, which makes this an excellent path when a bonus is active.

My Thoughts

This is a fair promotion rather than a blockbuster one.

Because the top tier lands right at the 2 cent valuation, I would only buy Avios here if I had a concrete redemption ready to book. If that redemption is Finnair business class to Helsinki or Cathay Pacific to Hong Kong, then buying Avios at roughly 2 cents to unlock a seat worth 3.38 cents or more is an easy win.

If you are only chasing an economy seat to Europe, run the numbers first. As the Finnair economy example shows, cash can sometimes be the smarter option.

The Bottom Line

If you have a high-value redemption in mind, this Finnair Avios sale is a reasonable way to top up your balance. Just remember that you need a minimum balance of 50 Avios for the offer to appear, and you must log in to see your pricing and bonus. The promotion runs until October 5, 2026.

As always, I would not buy Avios without a specific redemption in mind. Award pricing and availability can change without notice, so confirm both before you buy.

Happy travels!
