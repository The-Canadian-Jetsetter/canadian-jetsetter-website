The Best Aeroplan Redemptions For Major Canadian Cities (Free Mini-Guide)

If you want to fly in a lie-flat business class seat on Aeroplan points, one thing becomes obvious fast: some award routes are far easier to book than others.

Take Toronto to Frankfurt. It is one of the most reliable business class redemptions in the country because there is so much flight volume between the two cities on Lufthansa. Now try to book Toronto to Taipei or Beijing. Suddenly it is a different game, with less inventory and a lot more competition for every seat that opens up.

That difference is exactly why I put together a new free mini-guide: The Best Aeroplan Redemptions For Major Canadian Cities.

Header Image Source: Air Canada.

What The Guide Covers

Source: Air Canada.

Instead of a generic list of “sweet spots” that may not even apply to where you live, this short guide breaks everything down by departure city. Whether you are flying out of Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver, Calgary, or Ottawa, you will find the best premium cabin redemptions you can actually target from your home airport.

For each route, you get:

  • An availability rating (Easy, Moderate, or Competitive) so you know how hard a seat is to find before you even start searching
  • The points and fees you should expect to pay
  • Notes on each redemption, including which airline and cabin you are booking and the best season to look

My Thoughts

Points are only worth something if you can actually book the trip you want. Knowing which routes are easy wins and which ones take patience is half the battle, and it is the kind of thing that usually takes years of trial and error to learn. This guide puts it all in one place so you can skip straight to the good part.

The Bottom Line

If Aeroplan is your program, or you are just trying to figure out where to point your credit card spending, this is a great place to start. It is free, it is quick to read, and it might just help you book your next dream trip in business class.

One quick note on points: award availability, taxes, and fees can change without warning, and the numbers in the guide reflect what has typically been available rather than a guarantee. Always confirm the details at the time of booking.
