Marriott Bonvoy to Aeroplan: Get 15% More Points Before October 31

If you have a pile of hotel points sitting idle, Air Canada is running the one Marriott transfer bonus worth paying attention to this year. Through Aeroplan, you can convert Marriott Bonvoy points and come away with 15% more than the standard rate. The offer runs until October 31, and it is the only promotion of its kind on the calendar in 2026.

Transfer bonuses like this are how a stranded hotel balance becomes a business class seat. But the headline number does not tell you the whole story, so let me walk through exactly what you get, what it is worth, and the one rule that decides whether you get the full value or leave points on the table.

This offer is only valid on conversions made between October 1 and October 31, 2026 (Eastern time).

How the offer works

Source: Aeroplan.

There are three layers stacked on top of each other here.

First, the standard conversion. Marriott Bonvoy points normally transfer to Aeroplan at 3:1, so every 3 Marriott points become 1 Aeroplan point.

Second, the Marriott bonus. For every 60,000 Marriott points you convert, Marriott throws in an extra 5,000 Aeroplan points on top.

Third, the 15% Aeroplan bonus. On top of all of that, Aeroplan adds 15% more points. And the great part is that the 15% applies to the Marriott bonus points too, not just the base conversion.

Let me break down the math

The easiest way to see it is to walk through a 60,000-point transfer one step at a time.

Step 1. The standard conversion. Marriott converts to Aeroplan at 3:1, so you divide by 3. 60,000 ÷ 3 = 20,000 Aeroplan.

Step 2. The Marriott bonus. Marriott adds 5,000 Aeroplan points for every full 60,000 you convert. That is exactly one block here, so you get another 5,000. 20,000 + 5,000 = 25,000 Aeroplan.

Step 3. The 15% Aeroplan bonus. The 15% applies to that entire 25,000, including the Marriott bonus points. 25,000 × 15% = 3,750 Aeroplan.

Step 4. The total. 20,000 + 5,000 + 3,750 = 28,750 Aeroplan.

So 60,000 Marriott Bonvoy points become 28,750 Aeroplan points.

Now, what does that do to the usual 3:1 rate? Work out how much Aeroplan you get per Marriott point (28,750 ÷ 60,000 = 0.48), then scale it back to the familiar 3 (0.48 × 3 = 1.44). Normally 3 Marriott points get you 1 Aeroplan point.

With this promo, those same 3 Marriott points get you 1.44 Aeroplan points, an effective 3:1.44 rate. Line the totals up side by side and it is the same story: 28,750 Aeroplan instead of 20,000 is about 44% more points for the exact same balance.

The one rule that matters: transfer in blocks of 60,000

Source: Marriott Bonvoy.

The 5,000-point Marriott bonus is awarded per full 60,000 points converted, so the 3:1.44 rate only holds when you transfer in clean multiples of 60,000.

Convert an odd number and you still get the 15%, but you miss the 5,000 bonus on anything under the next 60,000 threshold. Move 30,000 points, for example, and you end up with 11,500 Aeroplan, which works out to about 3:1.15 instead of 3:1.44. Still better than standard, but noticeably worse than the full deal.

If you are going to do this, line your transfer up to a multiple of 60,000 (60,000, 120,000, 180,000, and so on) to squeeze out every bonus point. You can move up to 240,000 Marriott points per day.

Is it worth it?

Source: Air Canada.

Marriott Bonvoy points are generally valued at about 0.8 cents CAD each, and I value Aeroplan points at roughly 2 cents CAD each. Run the 60,000-point example through that:

Those 60,000 Marriott points are worth about $480 CAD. The 28,750 Aeroplan points you get back are worth about $575 CAD. You are turning $480 CAD of hotel value into $575 CAD of airline value, a gain of roughly $95 CAD, or about 20% more value in your pocket.

Put another way, the promo lets you pick up Aeroplan points for about 1.67 cents each. That is below what an Aeroplan point is worth, which is exactly what you want.

Before you hit transfer

Source: Marriott Bonvoy.

A few things to check so the conversion goes through cleanly:

Your name on your Aeroplan account has to match your Marriott Bonvoy account name exactly. If they do not match, the transfer will not process. You can convert online or by calling 1-800-450-4442. The minimum transfer is 3,000 Marriott points, and the daily maximum is 240,000.

One thing to plan around: the bonus Aeroplan points can take 8 to 10 weeks to land after the promotion ends. The base points post quickly, but do not count on the bonus for a booking you need to make this month.

My Thoughts

Many hotel-to-airline transfers can be a bad idea, because you typically lose value moving points from a program where they are worth more into one where they are worth less. A bonus like this is the exception. At an effective 3:1.44 with the value working in your favour, this is a reasonable way to top up an Aeroplan balance that is sitting just short of the award you actually want.

I would not transfer speculatively. Hotel points are flexible and Aeroplan transfers are one-way, so only move what you have a real plan for. But if you are 20,000 or 30,000 points away from a business class seat and you have Marriott points gathering dust, this is the push to get you there.

The Bottom Line

The Marriott Bonvoy to Aeroplan bonus runs until October 31 and turns the usual 3:1 rate into an effective 3:1.44 when you transfer in blocks of 60,000. That is about 44% more Aeroplan points and roughly 20% more value than you started with. If you need a top-up and have a redemption in mind, it is worth doing before the end of the month.

As always, confirm the award space and pricing you are after before you transfer, since conversions cannot be reversed and award availability can change at any time.

Happy travels!
