✈️🇨🇦 Flights from $180 + Airline Sales & a New Rewards Program!

Hey Travellers!

There is a new rewards program on the block… (points nerds, unite!) 😁

After months of testing and refining, Air Transat has finally launched Staria, its new rewards program. This is a very interesting program that differs a bit from what we are used to with Aeroplan, WestJet Rewards, etc.

Staria, Air Transat’s new rewards program. Source: Air Transat.

At its core, Staria is quite simple. There is no complicated award chart and no status tiers to chase. It is free to join, anyone can sign up, and you start earning on your very next Air Transat or Porter flight.

The whole thing is built on three pieces:

  • ⭐ Points are your main currency. You earn them on flights, packages, and extras, then use them to knock money off a future trip. Note that they work like a fixed-value rebate rather than a traditional award chart.
  • 🌟 Stars are a separate layer you collect by completing little “missions” like finishing your profile or logging in once a month. Rack up enough and you unlock perks like a free checked bag, free seat selection, or priority boarding, all without spending a dollar.
  • 🪐 Constellations are the family-friendly feature. You can pool your points with up to 8 other people into one shared balance, so a whole group reaches free and discounted travel way faster than any one person could alone.

Intrigued? If you want to learn more, you can read about it in my recent blog post by clicking here. Alternatively, Jer and I discussed it in last week’s podcast, which you can watch below.

A new rewards program is great news for Canadians, as you can earn and redeem points by spending money you would have spent anyway on a trip. If you are a frequent Air Transat flyer, or you will be travelling with them in the near future, definitely make sure to sign up. You can do so by clicking the button below.

Cheers!
