✈️🇨🇦 Flights from $255 + AMEX Marriott Bonvoy Shake-Up & Your Aeroplan Points Can Expire?!

Hey Travellers,

Did you hear the latest AMEX news? 👀💳

Source: American Express Canada.

American Express Canada just announced a major shake-up to its Marriott Bonvoy credit card lineup, and all of it takes effect February 9, 2027. Here is what is changing:

  • 💳 A higher annual fee on the flagship card
  • 🆕 A brand new no annual fee card joining the family
  • 🥇 A status bump to Gold Elite
  • 🏨 A new spending requirement before you earn your annual free night

I broke down every change, who comes out ahead, who needs to rethink the card, and exactly what to do before year end in my blog below.

Click here to learn more →

Source: Aeroplan.

In less welcome news, after more than five years on pause, Aeroplan points expiry is back as of November 30, 2026.

This is not a fresh 18 months for everyone. The inactivity clock has been running the entire time, so if your account has gone untouched for 18 months or longer, your points could start disappearing the day the pause lifts. I know this probably will not apply to most of you reading this, but if you have a friend or family member who has not touched their Aeroplan account in a few years, it is worth giving them a heads up.

The good news is that it takes just one small transaction to reset the clock. An Uber Eats order, a quick points transfer, a Starbucks top-up. I covered the easiest ways to stay active, how to check your own risk in about two minutes, and the single move that takes expiry off the table for good.
