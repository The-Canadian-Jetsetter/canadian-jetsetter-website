✈️🇨🇦 Flights from $361 + Air Canada’s “Basic” Shakeup

Hey Travellers,

If you ever fly Air Canada in premium economy or business class, this one is worth your attention. Back in the summer, Air Canada quietly rolled out basic business class and basic premium economy fares on select international routes, including flights to Mexico, Latin America, and some transatlantic destinations.

This week’s news is that they are bringing those same stripped-down premium fares to North American routes, covering flights within Canada and between Canada and the US.

Air Canada’s Premium Economy and Business Class Cabins. Source: Air Canada.

The catch is right there in the word “basic.” These fares strip out refunds, free advanced seat selection, your second checked bag, and eUpgrades, and basic business even loses access to the Air Canada Signature Suite in Toronto and Vancouver.

In this week’s podcast episode, Jer and I break down exactly what you keep, what you lose, and why this change looks less like “more choice” and more like a nudge toward the Aeroplan credit card world.

And that is just the first topic. In Episode 10 we also cover:

  • WestJet and Porter upgrades: why you can still bid for an upgrade on even their cheapest fares (unlike Air Canada), and which upgrade methods actually earn you points and qualifying spend.
  • Our first ever mailbag, where we answer your questions on:
    • The Marriott free night award mistake that quietly costs people a free fifth night, and the top-up trick that unlocks far nicer properties.
    • The Marriott Bonvoy card overhaul, and the deadline that decides whether your next free night comes with a spending catch.
    • The 30% RBC Avion transfer bonus ending soon, whether you should jump on it, and the Avios move most Canadians overlook.
    • Haneda or Narita for getting into Tokyo, and what ZIPAIR’s new 2027 route really means for your wallet.
    • The best points redemptions we have booked recently, and exactly how we found them.

See below to watch the episode, and enjoy! 😁

And now… the actual flight deals. Think round-trip fares from $361 to southern destinations, $401 in Canada, $462 to Europe, $1,334 to Oceania, and Aeroplan redemptions in Economy class starting at 22,600 points + fees one-way.

As always, prices and availability can change quickly. If something lines up with your 2026-27 travel plans, it is worth taking a closer look sooner rather than later.

Let’s dive into this week’s best fares. ✈️
