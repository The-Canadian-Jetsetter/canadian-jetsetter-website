WestJet's Winter Sun Schedule Grows, With Western Canada Leading the Way

If you are already thinking about escaping the cold this winter, WestJet has given you more options to work with. The airline has announced its largest ever winter schedule to Latin America and the Caribbean, with roughly 12 per cent more capacity year over year between Canada and popular sun destinations. At peak, that works out to as many as 1,252 weekly flights across 88 non-stop routes.

The headline number is nice, but the more useful detail for most travellers is where the growth is actually happening. This year it is heavily concentrated in Western Canada, and several smaller markets are getting service they have not had before.

Image Header Source: WestJet.

Where the New Flights Are Going

Here is how the growth breaks down by city.

Saskatoon sees the biggest jump on a percentage basis, with capacity up 77 per cent. WestJet is adding a weekly flight to both Puerto Vallarta and Cancun, moving those routes from four to five times weekly during peak periods.

Regina is close behind at 64 per cent growth, and for the first time will get non-stop flights to Los Cabos.

Edmonton grows by 34 per cent, with twice as many weekly flights to Los Cabos and to Montego Bay, Jamaica compared to last winter.

Winnipeg picks up 26 per cent more capacity, with added frequencies to Liberia (Costa Rica), Cancun, Puerto Vallarta and Los Cabos, plus brand new service to Belize City and to Punta Cana in the Dominican Republic.

Calgary rounds things out at 18 per cent growth. As WestJet's hub, it is getting twice-daily flights to Los Cabos, more daily flights to Cancun, and a new non-stop route to Merida, Mexico.

If you are used to connecting through Calgary or Toronto to reach the beach, some of these new non-stops could save you a leg.

The Bigger Story: Calgary to Sao Paulo

Beyond the sun routes, WestJet is also launching the only direct service between Western Canada and South America. Starting this winter, the airline will fly non-stop between Calgary and Sao Paulo, Brazil, a flight of about 14 hours.

One thing worth noting is that the route has already been trimmed. It was originally set to start on November 8, 2026, but a couple of months ago WestJet scaled it back. The service is now scheduled to begin on December 15, 2026 and run until February 15, 2027, so it is shaping up as a seasonal winter route rather than the longer run first announced.

What makes this more interesting than a single route is the connection to LATAM. Through that partnership, WestJet says travellers will be able to book more than 60 additional destinations across South America on a single itinerary. For anyone who has tried to piece together a trip deeper into the continent, that kind of interline booking can remove a lot of friction.

The Sao Paulo route joins WestJet's existing Dreamliner network, which this winter also brings back select widebody flights to Cancun, Puerto Vallarta and Maui. Year round, the Dreamliner continues to serve London Heathrow, Paris and Tokyo, with seasonal summer service to cities like Rome, Seoul, Dublin, Edinburgh and Barcelona.

My Thoughts

As someone based in Winnipeg, it is good to see our airport included in this round rather than left to connect through everything. That is the part that stands out most to me. New non-stops to Belize City and Punta Cana mean you can skip transferring through Calgary, which is WestJet's hub, and you also avoid the Toronto, Montreal or Vancouver connection you would typically face if you flew Air Canada instead. Cutting out that extra leg on each end makes a winter trip feel a lot more doable.

The Sao Paulo launch is the part I will be watching most closely. Western Canada has never had a direct link to South America, and pairing it with LATAM's network opens up trips that used to mean routing through the United States.

The Bottom Line

This is a meaningful expansion, and the fact that so much of it lands in Western Canada and smaller prairie markets is a nice change of pace. Whether you are chasing sun in Mexico and the Caribbean or eyeing that new Sao Paulo route, it is worth checking WestJet.com to see if any of the new flights line up with where you want to go this winter.

Happy travels!
