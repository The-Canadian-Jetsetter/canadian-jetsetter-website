Flying Blue June 2026 Promo: Economy & Premium Economy to Europe from Toronto

Flying Blue is the loyalty program for Air France, KLM, and select partner airlines. Members earn miles when flying or through partners, and those miles can be redeemed for flights, upgrades, and other perks.

One of the most valuable aspects of the program is the monthly Promo Rewards, which offer discounted award tickets on select routes. New Promo Rewards are released on the first day of each month, with discounts of up to 25 percent.

For June, Toronto is featured with Extra Exclusive deals to Europe — both Economy and Premium Economy at a 25% discount. That's Economy from 18,750 miles one way (was 25,000) and Premium Economy from 30,000 miles one way (was 40,000). Note that these deals are only available to Flying Blue Extra members, which we'll explain below.

Travel dates are available until November 30, 2026.

Read on to learn more, or visit Air France's website below to search for redemptions!

Toronto Promo

Two cabin classes from Toronto to Europe are available at a 25% discount this month:

  • Economy: 18,750 miles one way (was 25,000)
  • Premium Economy: 30,000 miles one way (was 40,000)

Both are solid options for anyone looking to get to Europe without burning through a large chunk of their points balance, whether you want a straightforward economy seat or a more comfortable Premium Economy experience.

These redemptions are marked as "Extra Exclusive," meaning they are only available to Flying Blue Extra subscribers. If you're an Extra member, you'll be able to search for available dates directly on the Air France website.

Flying Blue Extra

To access this Toronto deal, you'll need to be a Flying Blue Extra member. Flying Blue Extra is a paid annual membership designed for frequent Air France and KLM flyers. It provides benefits such as:

  • Access to Extra Exclusive Promo Rewards
  • Bonus miles on paid flights
  • A set number of lounge passes each year

If you fly Air France or KLM regularly, the Extra program can quickly pay for itself and unlocks more opportunities to use your miles for valuable long-haul flights.

Don't Have Enough Miles? Buy Miles with Up to 45% Off — Ends June 7

If you're short on miles, there's still time to take advantage of Flying Blue's extended buy-miles promotion, which runs until June 7, 2026.

Flying Blue is offering up to 45% off when you purchase miles, on a tiered structure based on how many you buy:

  • 4,000 – 10,000 Miles: 35% off
  • 12,000 – 22,000 Miles: 40% off
  • 24,000+ Miles: 45% off

Note: Some members may receive different tier breakdowns based on their account.

At the highest discount level, the cost works out to approximately 2.3 cents CAD per mile — which can make topping up your balance a very attractive option, especially when you already have award availability confirmed.

You can also transfer AMEX Membership Rewards points to Flying Blue at a 1:1 rate as another way to top up your balance without purchasing miles outright.

How Flying Blue Reward Tickets Work

Flying Blue Promo Reward tickets are easy to use but have a few conditions:

  • Tickets must be booked directly by Flying Blue members and are payable in miles.
  • Flights are operated by Air France and KLM, and may include connecting flights within Europe through Paris or Amsterdam.
  • Taxes and carrier surcharges are not covered by miles and must be paid separately.
  • Reward flights do not earn additional miles or elite credit.

For families traveling with infants or minors traveling alone, it is best to contact Flying Blue Customer Service before booking.

A Tip for Canadians

If you collect American Express Membership Rewards points, Flying Blue is a flexible option for redemption. Points transfer 1:1 to Flying Blue, making these Promo Rewards particularly attractive for Canadians who want to maximize value.

Final Thoughts

Economy flights to Europe from Toronto for just 18,750 miles one way, and Premium Economy for 30,000 miles one way, are both strong deals — particularly for Flying Blue Extra members who can unlock them. And if you're short on miles, the buy-miles promotion running until June 7 gives you a timely opportunity to top up at up to 45% off.

Availability is limited, so if Europe has been on your radar, it's a good idea to act quickly and lock in your seat.
