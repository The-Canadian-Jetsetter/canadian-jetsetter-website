✈️🇨🇦 Flights from $186, New International Routes from Canada & Flying Blue Promotions!

Hey Travellers!

New destinations upon new destinations! ✈️

Air Canada’s new destinations beginning in 2027.

Air Canada just announced five new destinations beginning summer 2027. The new destinations are:

  • Toronto to Oslo, Norway 🇳🇴 (June 2nd – October 10th, 2027)
  • Toronto to Shannon, Ireland 🇮🇪 (June 10th – October 2nd, 2027)
  • Montréal to Dubrovnik, Croatia 🇭🇷 (May 11th – October 23rd, 2027)
  • Montréal to Basel, Switzerland 🇨🇭 (June 16th – October 23rd, 2027)
  • Vancouver to Guangzhou, China 🇨🇳 (Year-round, begins May 4th, 2027)

These are quite exciting routes and it will be great to have direct connections with these destinations.

On another note, Air France-KLM Flying Blue launched their new monthly promo for September 2026 and it includes three Canadian cities! Reminder that new Flying Blue Promo Rewards go live on the first day of each month, with discounts of up to 25%.

Three Canadian cities featured in Flying Blue’s September 2026 Monthly Promotion.

For September, the three Canadian cities featured with discounted Promo Rewards to Europe are Montréal, Toronto, and Vancouver.

Montréal is offered in Economy, while Toronto and Vancouver are both offered in Premium Economy. The Montréal and Vancouver deals are open to all Flying Blue members, while the Toronto deal is an Extra Exclusive, meaning you need a Flying Blue Extra membership to book it.

These September Promo Rewards must be booked by September 30th, 2026, for travel through February 28th, 2027, subject to availability.

In addition to this, there is a limited-time Flying Blue promotion where until September 16th, 2026, you can receive up to 45% off when you purchase miles. If you find a great redemption from one of the featured cities above and need to top up your miles, this is a great promotion to take advantage of.
