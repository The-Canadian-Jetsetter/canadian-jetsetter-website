Big Changes and a New Card: What Is Happening to Amex Marriott Bonvoy in 2027

If you hold an Amex Marriott Bonvoy card, or you have been thinking about applying for one, there is a significant shake-up on the way.

American Express Canada has announced a series of changes to its Marriott Bonvoy credit card lineup, all taking effect on February 9, 2027. Some of it is good, some of it isn't, but we'll get into that. Also, there is an entirely new card joining the family. On top of that, the timing of when you apply could save you a full round of spending.

Here is everything you need to know.

Header Image Source: American Express Canada.

What Is Changing on the Marriott Bonvoy American Express Card

The flagship consumer card is getting a mix of upgrades and new requirements.

The annual fee on the Marriott Bonvoy American Express Card is increasing from $120 CAD to $150 CAD. This takes effect on your first annual fee billing date on or after February 9, 2027.

On the upside, cardholders will now receive complimentary Marriott Bonvoy Gold Elite Status, an upgrade from the Silver Elite Status the card carried before. Gold Elite unlocks a few more valuable perks, including a 25 percent bonus on points earned on eligible stays, 2 p.m. late checkout, and enhanced room upgrades based on availability. One important caveat worth flagging: Gold status still does not include complimentary breakfast. That benefit only arrives at the higher tiers, so do not expect it with this bump.

The change that will matter most to a lot of people is the free night award. Previously, the card handed you an Annual Free Night Award simply for keeping the card past your anniversary. Going forward, you will need to spend $3,000 CAD on the card before that free night is issued. The award itself is unchanged in value and covers a one-night stay in a standard room at a redemption level of up to 35,000 points.

The rest of the earn structure stays the same. You will continue to earn 5X Marriott Bonvoy points at participating Marriott hotels and 2X on everything else, and you will still receive 15 Elite Night Credits each year toward your next status tier.

The Timing Matters: Apply Before December 31, 2026

Here is where it pays to move quickly. When the new $3,000 CAD spending requirement kicks in depends on when you applied for the card. If you apply before December 31, 2026, you can lock in an extra year of the free night award under the old rules, which means one more year without having to hit the spend.

And if you already hold the card, or you applied for it before today, you are still in line to receive one more free night award the old way.

American Express has shared specific examples of exactly when the spending clock starts depending on your situation. The short version is this: if the free night has been a big reason you hold this card, getting your application in before the end of 2026 is the smart move.

A New No Annual Fee Option: The Marriott Bonvoy Bright Card

Source: American Express Canada.

Alongside the changes to the existing cards, Amex is launching an entirely new product: the Marriott Bonvoy Bright American Express Card, also arriving on February 9, 2027. The headline feature is that it carries no annual fee, which makes it the most accessible way into the Marriott Bonvoy program through Amex.

Here is what the Bright card offers:

  • 3X Marriott Bonvoy points at participating Marriott hotels, 2X on dining, and 1.25X on all other eligible purchases
  • Complimentary Marriott Bonvoy Silver Elite Status
  • 5 Elite Night Credits each year to get you started toward a higher tier

It is a solid entry-level option if you want to dip into Marriott Bonvoy without committing to a fee, or if you are building toward status and want a few Elite Night Credits working in your favour from day one.

What About the Business Card

If you carry the Marriott Bonvoy Business American Express Card, there is good news: your annual fee stays put at $150 CAD. You get the same upgrade to Gold Elite Status, and you keep earning 5X at Marriott hotels, 3X on eligible gas, dining, and travel, and 2X everywhere else.

As with the consumer card, the Annual Free Night Award now arrives after $3,000 CAD in spending, and you continue to receive 15 Elite Night Credits a year.

My Thoughts

The free night award is good for a stay of up to 35,000 points, which I would put somewhere in the range of $200 to $400 CAD in real value depending on where and when you book. The honest question every cardholder needs to ask is whether $3,000 CAD in spending on this card is worth that free night going forward.

For me, the answer is still yes. I stay in enough Marriott properties and I hold Titanium status, so the card continues to earn its place in my wallet. But I will be upfront: for a lot of people I know, this change shifts the value proposition. If you were holding the card mainly for the easy annual free night and you do not put much spend through it, the new requirement is worth thinking hard about.

The move to Gold status is a nice step up, but the missing free breakfast keeps it from being the slam dunk it might first appear to be.

The Bottom Line

The Marriott Bonvoy lineup is becoming more flexible, with a new no annual fee card at the entry level and a status bump across the existing cards. The trade-off is a higher fee on the consumer card and a new spending hurdle for the free night.

If that free night matters to you, apply before December 31, 2026 to buy yourself an extra year under the old rules. And if you already hold the card, you have one more free night coming the old way.

Happy travels!
