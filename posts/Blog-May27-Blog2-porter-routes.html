4 New Porter Routes That Just Launched in Canada!

Porter Airlines has had a busy month!

Four new domestic routes quietly launched over the past few weeks, and if you haven't heard about them yet, here's why each one is worth getting excited about.

1. Ottawa to Kelowna

This one launched May 13 and it's a first for Canadian aviation: Porter is the only airline flying this route non-stop.

If you've ever tried to get from Ottawa to the Okanagan, you know it usually means a connection through Vancouver or Calgary and a good chunk of your day eaten up in airports. Now you can leave Ottawa in the morning and land in Kelowna before noon, with a glass of complimentary wine or cup of coffee waiting for you on board.

For Okanagan locals, it's an equally exciting development. Direct access to Canada's capital, plus easy connections east to Halifax and St. John's, opens up a whole new world of routing options.

2. Hamilton to Winnipeg

Porter's Hamilton hub has been growing at an impressive pace since it launched in mid-2025, and the Winnipeg route is one of its most significant additions yet. Non-stop service between Hamilton (YHM) and Winnipeg (YWG) started May 14, running up to six round trips per week.

What makes this exciting is what it means for travellers in the Greater Hamilton and Niagara area. Getting to Winnipeg used to mean driving to Toronto Pearson and dealing with everything that comes with it. Now you can park at a smaller, easier airport, skip the congestion, and fly direct. Hamilton now serves 14 non-stop destinations, which at this point makes it a legitimate travel hub in its own right.

3. Hamilton to St. John's

Launched the same day as the Winnipeg route (May 14), Porter's Hamilton to St. John's service gives Southern Ontario a direct line to the East Coast for the first time. Daily flights are expected to be running by late June.

Newfoundland is one of Canada's most underrated travel destinations, and historically, getting there from the Hamilton or Niagara region meant a detour through Toronto. That friction is now gone. On the flip side, St. John's travellers gain direct access to Southern Ontario and the Niagara region, which is a genuinely useful connection for both leisure and visiting family.

4. Toronto Pearson to Quebec City

Porter resumed non-stop service between Toronto Pearson (YYZ) and Quebec City (YQB) on May 12, with 12 weekly round trips on the E195-E2. This complements the existing Billy Bishop to Quebec City route, meaning Toronto travellers now have two convenient departure points depending on where they're coming from.

What makes this one particularly interesting is the connectivity it unlocks. Flying through YYZ opens up onward connections to Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Winnipeg, and Kelowna heading west. And for Quebec City travellers looking to go even further, there are connecting flights through Air Transat's European network, including Rome, Venice, Amsterdam, and more. Not bad for a regional route.

If a long weekend in Old Quebec wasn't already tempting enough, being able to tag it onto a European trip or use YQB as a stepping stone makes it even more compelling.

The Bottom Line

Porter has had a remarkable stretch of growth, and these four routes are just part of the story. The airline is clearly doubling down on Hamilton as a serious travel hub, while also expanding out of its core bases in Ottawa and Toronto.

Beyond Canada, Porter has been launching new international routes at a steady clip, from destinations in the U.S. to vacation-worthy spots in the south. And there's more to come: on June 15, 2026, Porter will launch its first flights out of Montreal's brand new Montreal Metropolitan Airport (MET), which is shaping up to be one of the more exciting developments in Canadian aviation this year.

It's a good time to be flying Porter!
