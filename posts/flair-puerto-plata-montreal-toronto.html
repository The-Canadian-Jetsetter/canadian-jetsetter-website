Flair Launches New Puerto Plata Service From Montreal and Toronto

Flair has another winter sun route on the way, and this time it is a brand-new destination for the airline. Starting in mid-December, Flair Airlines will fly nonstop from both Montreal and Toronto to Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic, adding one of the Caribbean's most popular beach spots to its network.

For travellers in Quebec and Ontario looking for an affordable escape from the cold, this is a welcome addition heading into winter. Here is what we know so far.

Header Image Source: Flair Airlines.

The New Route

The new service connects Puerto Plata International Airport (POP) with two of Canada's biggest markets. Flair will fly from Montreal (YUL) starting December 18, twice per week, with fares starting at $279 CAD one way. Toronto (YYZ) service follows on December 19, operating up to twice per week, with fares starting at $249 CAD one way.

Route Launch date Frequency Starting fare (one way)
Montreal (YUL) to Puerto Plata (POP) Dec. 18, 2026 2x weekly $279 CAD
Toronto (YYZ) to Puerto Plata (POP) Dec. 19, 2026 Up to 2x weekly $249 CAD
Montreal (YUL) to Puerto Vallarta (PVR) Dec. 16, 2026 1x weekly $269 CAD

You will also spot a third route in the table above. Alongside Puerto Plata, Flair is launching nonstop service from Montreal to Puerto Vallarta (PVR) on Mexico's Pacific coast starting December 16.

Puerto Plata sits on the Dominican Republic's north coast and is known for its beaches and resorts, along with a historic city centre, mountains, and adventure options for travellers who want more than a poolside week. Flair is offering both air-only fares and packages through Flair Vacations, so you can book just the flight or bundle it with a hotel.

As always with low-cost carriers, the starting fare is a starting point rather than the final number. Base fares climb once you add bags, seat selection, and other extras, so the real cost depends on how you pack and how you fly.

Two Very Different Flair Networks

Puerto Plata lands very differently in each city.

For Montreal, it is another step in a fast build-out from a small base. Flair now serves five destinations from YUL: Cancun, Puerto Plata, Puerto Vallarta, and Punta Cana for the sun, plus Vancouver as its lone domestic route. That new Puerto Vallarta service also makes Montreal its fifth Canadian city serving the Mexican Pacific destination. Just a couple of months ago Flair added a Montreal to Punta Cana route for this winter, so the pace at YUL is hard to miss.

Toronto is a different story. Flair flies to more than 20 destinations from YYZ, so Puerto Plata is one more sun option on top of an already deep network. That network leans heavily on domestic routes, with service to cities like Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Halifax, and Winnipeg, plus smaller markets such as Thunder Bay, Moncton, and Charlottetown, alongside U.S. and Mexico sun spots like Orlando, Puerto Vallarta, and Montego Bay. The short version: Puerto Plata is a bigger deal for Montreal travellers than for Toronto, where Flair already offers plenty of ways to get out of town.

The Bottom Line

A brand-new nonstop option to the Dominican Republic is good news for anyone planning a winter getaway from Montreal or Toronto, and Flair's entry could put some pressure on fares to the sun. Whether it ends up being the cheapest way to go will come down to the all-in price once you add everything you need, but more choice and another nonstop route is a solid win.
