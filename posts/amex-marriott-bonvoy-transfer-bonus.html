Amex Is Offering a 30% Transfer Bonus to Marriott Bonvoy

American Express Canada has brought back one of its recurring promotions: a 30% bonus when you transfer Membership Rewards points over to Marriott Bonvoy. If you have been sitting on a pile of Membership Rewards points and you have a hotel stay or an award flight in mind, this one is worth a look.

The offer is live from August 4 to September 3, 2026. That is a decent window to sit with the decision and work out whether it lines up with an actual trip. Just keep in mind up front that these transfers are permanent, so this is only a good deal if you truly have a use for the points.

Header Image Source: Marriott Bonvoy

What the 30% Bonus Actually Gets You

Source: American Express

Normally, Canadian Membership Rewards points transfer to Marriott Bonvoy at a rate of 1,000 to 1,200 (1:1.2). During this promotion, that jumps to an effective 1,000 to 1,560 (1:1.56) once the bonus is applied.

Here is what that looks like across a few transfer amounts:

  • 1,000 Membership Rewards points become 1,560 Marriott Bonvoy points
  • 10,000 Membership Rewards points become 15,600 Marriott Bonvoy points
  • 50,000 Membership Rewards points become 78,000 Marriott Bonvoy points
  • 100,000 Membership Rewards points become 156,000 Marriott Bonvoy points

One thing that trips people up: the transfer screen itself only reflects the regular 1,000 to 1,200 rate. Your bonus is not calculated in that moment. It lands as a separate credit later on, and Amex says to allow up to eight weeks after September 3 for it to appear in your Marriott account. If your balance looks light right after transferring, that is expected.

How to Transfer Your Points

Source: American Express

The process is straightforward:

  1. Log in at membershiprewards.ca
  2. Link your Marriott Bonvoy account if you have not already. You need to be enrolled in Marriott Bonvoy to take part, and you can sign up at marriott.com if needed
  3. Transfer in increments of 100 points, with a minimum of 1,000 points per transfer

A few other details are worth knowing. Amex allows up to 48 hours for a transfer to complete, though these often clear well before that. It is a good idea to confirm your name and address are entered identically across both accounts, since a mismatch is the usual culprit behind a stuck transfer. Each transfer maxes out at 999,990 points. And once your points land in Marriott Bonvoy they are locked in, with no path back to Membership Rewards, and the bonus portion will not count toward an Elite tier.

Is It Worth Transferring?

Source: Marriott Bonvoy

The honest answer is that it depends on what you plan to do with the points.

On a pure value-per-point basis, hotel transfers rarely come out on top. Membership Rewards points tend to go furthest when you send them straight to an airline program and put them toward a premium cabin seat, which usually beats what the same points return as hotel nights. Amex Canada lets you send Membership Rewards points to Aeroplan, the British Airways Club, and Air France KLM Flying Blue at 1:1, and to Cathay Pacific Asia Miles at a slightly lower rate of 1:0.75.

That said, if your flights are already covered, or you specifically need Marriott points to top up for a hotel stay, then a 30% bonus is a solid win on the hotel side. It is also worth remembering that most trips involve both flights and hotels, so there is often room for both.

Stretching Even Further Through Airline Partners

Here is where it gets interesting for the points crowd. Even if flights are your real goal, routing through Marriott Bonvoy can still make sense. Marriott partners with roughly 38 airline programs, and the mechanics of how it converts points are what make this worth a second look.

The base conversion is 3 Marriott points to 1 airline mile. On top of that, Marriott throws in a standing bonus of 5,000 extra miles for every 60,000 points you transfer. So 60,000 Marriott points get you 20,000 miles from the base rate, plus the 5,000 bonus, for 25,000 miles in total. That is why points people always talk about transferring in 60,000-point chunks. It is the sweet spot where the bonus kicks in.

Now layer the Amex promotion on top. At the boosted 1:1.56 rate, roughly 38,462 Membership Rewards points turn into 60,000 Marriott points, which in turn become 25,000 airline miles. At that effective rate, a lot of award sweet spots suddenly come within reach, including plenty of airline programs that Membership Rewards cannot transfer to on its own.

A couple of specifics worth knowing. If you are a United MileagePlus member, the standing bonus doubles to 10,000 miles per 60,000 points, so the same 60,000 Marriott points get you 30,000 United miles. A few programs are excluded from the 5,000-mile bonus entirely, including American Airlines AAdvantage, Delta SkyMiles, and Avianca LifeMiles, so for those the effective rate is just the straight 3:1. Marriott also caps daily transfers between 3,000 and 240,000 points, so a very large move may need to be spread across a few days.

The catch is that you should only go this route if you have a specific award in mind. Because these transfers are one-way, speculatively converting points and hoping to use them later is exactly how points end up stranded.

My Thoughts

Personally, I treat these Marriott transfer bonuses as a backup plan rather than the main event. My Membership Rewards points are usually earmarked for premium cabin flights through Aeroplan, where I feel I get the most out of them. But I have jumped on a bonus like this before when I needed to top up my Bonvoy balance for a specific hotel I had my eye on, and the extra 30% made the stay noticeably cheaper in points terms.

The one rule I hold myself to is that I never transfer until I know exactly what I am booking. Points sitting in Membership Rewards keep all of their flexibility. The moment they move to Marriott, that flexibility is gone.

The Bottom Line

The 30% transfer bonus from Membership Rewards to Marriott Bonvoy is a good opportunity if, and only if, you have a use for the points. Whether that is an aspirational hotel stay or an airline redemption through one of Marriott's transfer partners, the boosted rate simply gives you more to work with.

Just remember that the transfer is permanent, so only move your points once you have a plan. The offer runs until September 3, 2026, with bonus points arriving up to eight weeks afterward.

Happy travels!
