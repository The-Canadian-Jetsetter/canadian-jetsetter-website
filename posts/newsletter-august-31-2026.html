✈️🇨🇦 Flights from $179, a Porter Contest & Up to 5X Bonus Aeroplan Points!

Hey Travellers!

Let’s start the week with something fun! ✈️

Porter Airlines is running a social media contest where 7 winners total will each receive a round-trip flight for two anywhere Porter flies in PorterClassic.

View this post on Instagram

A post shared by The Canadian Jetsetter (@thecanadianjetsetter)

All you have to do is the following:

  • Follow @porterairlines on Instagram
  • Tag someone you’d bring along and tell them which sun destination you’d escape to together
  • Make sure you’re both following Porter
  • Repost Porter’s reel using the repost feature for an additional entry

This contest ends September 4th, 2026 at 11:59 PM E.T., so you have some time to enter.

Good luck, Jetsetters!

Source: Air Canada.
