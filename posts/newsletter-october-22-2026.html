Hey Travellers!

Did someone say… new credit card?! 💳

Now everyone’s wondering: what will this card be? Any guesses? 👀

If you’d like to hear two credit card nerds speculate on exactly that (plus a few other fun topics along the way), check out episode #6 of The Canadian Jetsetter Podcast. I’ll embed it below for easy listening.

On the topic of sales… 🏷️

The Porter Airlines 20th Birthday Sale, giving up to 30% off base fares with promo code HBD2PD, is ending tomorrow! (October 23rd, 2026).

  • WestJet: 20% off select base fares to all destinations within Canada, and select destinations within the U.S. and other Southern Destinations. Use the promo code 3Q9M2GZ. Book by October 23rd, 2026. Click here to learn more →

And now… the actual flight deals. Think round-trip fares from $177 in Canada, $599 to Europe, $615 to South America, $674 to Asia, $1,289 to Oceania, and Aeroplan redemptions starting at 14,800 points + fees one-way.

As always, prices and availability can change quickly. If something lines up with your 2026-27 travel plans, it is worth taking a closer look sooner rather than later.
