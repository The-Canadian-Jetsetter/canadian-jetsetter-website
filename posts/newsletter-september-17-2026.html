✈️🇨🇦 Flights from $176, Week 3 of Aeroplan’s Sip-Tember & Icelandair Sale Extended!

Hey Travellers!

We’re currently on Week 3 of Sip-Tember!

If you don’t remember this from last week’s newsletter, there’s a promotion between Aeroplan and Starbucks where you can earn bonuses every week if you complete specific purchases in specific time frames. Plus, you’ll get contest entries for a chance to win 100,000 Aeroplan points.

Source: Starbucks / Aeroplan.

For this week (September 15th – 21st, 2026), earn 1 contest entry and 25 bonus Aeroplan points when you make a minimum $12+ pre-tax purchase after 12 p.m. with your linked account.

Also, if you order twice this week, you can earn a total of 50 Aeroplan points and 2 contest entries.

As I mentioned last time, this isn’t going to earn you a ton of points (unless you win the Aeroplan points contest!), but it’s a fun way to slightly bump up your points balance if you’re already ordering your cup of joe…or Pumpkin Spice Latte? 🎃

Check out the link below for more information on this week’s promotion + what’s happening for the rest of the month.

Good luck!

Source: Icelandair.

On another note, Icelandair’s sale from Canada to Iceland & Europe has been extended until September 21st, 2026!

Before they were promoting fares for $499 CAD; now they’re promoting fares from $599 CAD. That being said, they’re still offering some competitively priced itineraries, so I’d check them out if I were you.

Yes, it’s true you’ll need to have a layover in Reykjavík. Sometimes this is just for a couple hours, and sometimes it’s for almost 24 hours…but this means you get to explore this beautiful city for a day, which I highly recommend!
