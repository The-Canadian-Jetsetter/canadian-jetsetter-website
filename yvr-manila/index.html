Vancouver to Manila from $732 CAD Round Trip

Air China via Beijing, with dates over BC Spring Break 2027

The Deal at a Glance

Route
Vancouver (YVR) to Manila (MNL), round trip
Airline
Air China, 1 stop in Beijing (PEK)
Price
From $732 CAD round trip via online travel agencies, or about $757 CAD booking direct with Air China (Economy Flex fare)
Travel window
March 2027, including BC Spring Break (March 13 to 28, 2027 for Metro Vancouver districts)

Why You Won't See It on Google Flights

Google Flights is hiding Air China's own price, labelling it "price hidden because it might be incorrect." Because of that, this deal doesn't surface properly in search results.

It is bookable, though, both directly with Air China and through third-party booking sites.
